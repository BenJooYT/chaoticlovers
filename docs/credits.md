# Credits and forked looks

Where the visual borrowings come from, so nothing has to be reverse-engineered later.

## ben's corner

- **Layout:** [ray's "black/red vampire layout"](https://layouts.spacehey.com/layout?id=81296) on SpaceHey. What we took: the blood palette (`#5e0303`, `#b10707`), offset hard shadows (`4px 3px`), gradient heading bars, dashed struck-through links with red glow, the fixed photographic background, and the shake-on-hover gag. What we did **not** take: SpaceHey's DOM rules, the custom cursor file, the typewriter `h1`, and the original's embedded fonts.
- **Background:** `public/ben/media/vampire-forest.jpg`, the forest image the layout uses, self-hosted so a third-party host cannot break the page.
- **Fonts:** UnifrakturMaguntia, Cormorant Garamond and Grenze Gotisch, loaded from Google Fonts (all OFL). The layout's own Black Demon / My Chemical Romance / Goth Stencil TTFs are **not** bundled here; those files are fan fonts with unclear redistribution terms.
- **Blinkies:** see [blinkies.md](./blinkies.md), including the per-image maker credits.

## Rules

1. Credit the original author in the page footer and in this file when forking a look.
2. Recreate the _style_ in our own CSS. Do not paste a layout's whole stylesheet: most of it targets another site's markup and will not apply.
3. Do not bundle fonts lifted from a layout. Use an OFL equivalent or load from the foundry.
4. Third-party images get downloaded into the repo, never hotlinked.
